🏛️ US Government Blocks Foreign Access to Claude Fable 5 & Mythos 5 — Dario Pushes Back

The US Commerce Department ordered Anthropic to suspend all foreign-national access to its two most powerful models — including Anthropic's own foreign employees. The company disabled both models for everyone to comply. The cited reason: a potential narrow jailbreak in Fable 5's code-analysis capabilities. Anthropic called the order "a misunderstanding" and said it "does not adhere to principles of transparency or technical grounding." The Pentagon's CIO publicly backed the ban: "Some things are more important than revenue cycles, clickbait, and pre-IPO valuation." This is the first time the US has treated a frontier AI model like a controlled munition.
